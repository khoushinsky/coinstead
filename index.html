From statement to understanding

A calmer way to tend your finances.

Coinstead combines deterministic merchant matching, optional AI assistance, goal-based views, and planning tools in one private workspace.

↗

See growth in context

Your money tree and alignment score reflect the goals you define. Explore income, spending, savings, investments, debts, and recurring plans without presenting one universal definition of “healthy.”

◎

Import with less cleanup

Bring in CSV, plain-text, or text-readable PDF statements. Familiar merchants and your saved rules are applied first, while unresolved activity remains available for review.

✓

Confirm what the app cannot know

Use quick categories or review optional Apple Intelligence and ChatGPT suggestions. Suggestions are saved locally and remain subject to your approval.

⌁

Make merchant rules your own

Adjust categories and merchant mappings as your life changes. Saved rules can update closely matching transactions while preserving your explicit edits.

◇

Track debt alongside growth

Compare debt-payment priorities with savings and investment goals, see estimated payoff timing, and record transactions without treating projections as promises.

♧

Explore a purchase before making it

Garden Planning compares “continue as usual,” cash, financing, and wait-and-save scenarios against historical accumulation. Scenarios stay separate from your actual records and make their assumptions visible.

Purchase simulations are informational illustrations. They are not affordability decisions or professional financial advice.

Privacy you can understand

Your choices define the boundary.

Coinstead does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking. Financial records are stored on your iPhone. Optional sharing—such as a ChatGPT handoff or an export—happens only when you initiate it.
